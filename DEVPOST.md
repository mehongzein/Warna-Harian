## English description (paste ke bagian atas deskripsi submission)

```
Warna Harian is a daily color-mixing puzzle game on Reddit (Devvit Web).
Every day, players get one target color generated from the date seed — no
two days are ever the same. Tap the five base colors (Red, Yellow, Blue,
White, Black) to add drops to your mixture — the mixture is the average of
all your drops — and match the target color with as few drops as possible.
You win when the match reaches 96% or more. White lightens, black darkens,
and a solver-computed par (3–5 drops) sets the score to beat.

Daily features include a streak for playing consecutive days, a daily
top-10 leaderboard ranked by drops then time, and a shareable result card
with copy-score and share buttons. A 3-step first-time tutorial shows new
players exactly how to play, and the "Try an example" button highlights
the correct first drop of the day.
```

Optional closing line (akhir deskripsi):

```
Play today's color in r/warna_harian, share your result card in the
comments, and come back tomorrow to keep your streak alive.
```
